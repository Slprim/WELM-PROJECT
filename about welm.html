The Foundation of the Ministry

This ministry was founded by the Holy Spirit and was first named Global Impact Ministries with the tagline, Till Christ is formed in You. The ministry was inaugurated on the 5th of August, 2012 at the Busy Internet Café conference room on the Ring Road in Accra. In attendance to do the outdooring was Reverend Daniel Kissi-Adjabeng. About fifty people were in attendance that day. The ministry started with monthly conferences dubbed Faith and Fire Conferences. After meeting once at the above venue, venue for conferences was moved to the Paloma Hotel in the same area. After a couple of meetings there, the venue was again changed to the Fairview Hotel at Lapaz in January 2013. After a while, the ministry started having Sunday services at the Boston Hotel on 16th of June, 2013 with five people. Sunday services continued at the hall of the first senior pastor’s (Reverend Faith Joseph) parent, Mr. James Asiamah and Madam Kate Agyei with three constant members in attendance, namely Reverend Faith Joseph, Pastor Thomas Asante and Solomon Asamoah. After a while, due to some few challenges the Sunday services were put on hold and the conferences commenced again, still at Fairview hotel. In December, 2013 plans were underway to restart the Sunday services. It was later to be moved to the Kwashieman Cluster of Schools. In the course of time, the need to register the ministry as a legal entity, so that the church can have bank accounts, arose. This necessitated the ministry been registered as a Non-Governmental Organization. After conferring with the Holy Spirit, the Senior Pastor came out with name Words of Eternal Life Ministries (W.E.L.M), with the tagline Building you into the fullness of Christ to replace the name and tagline the ministry started with as at 2nd December, 2013. The church was formally registered on 23rd December, 2014
